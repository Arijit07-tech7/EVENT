01 / OVERVIEW

The Story Behind The Presentation

Partition was not only a political event. It was a human experience.

This presentation focuses on the lives of ordinary people whose homes, families, communities and livelihoods were deeply affected by the Partition of 1947.

Through historical context, personal stories, facts, migration, geography, social impact and rehabilitation, the presentation seeks to understand Partition through the experiences of common people.
