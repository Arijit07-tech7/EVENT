ABOUT THE PRESENTATION

Beyond Borders, Into Lives.

This presentation looks at Partition not only as a historical event, but through the experiences of ordinary people.

01

Understanding Partition

The presentation explores Punjab and Bengal before and during Partition, the causes and division, and the consequences experienced by common people.

It focuses on displacement, migration, separation of families, loss of homes and livelihoods, refugee life and the difficult process of rebuilding.

The central idea is to understand how a major historical transformation affected everyday lives and how people responded with resilience.

01

People

Stories and experiences of common people.

02

03

Homes, families, livelihoods and communities.

04

Rebuilding

Rehabilitation, recovery and new beginnings.
